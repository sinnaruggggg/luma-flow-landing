# Design System: LUNE FORM
**Site ID:** jewelry-brand

## 1. Identity
- Industry: Jewelry Brand
- Summary: A jewelry site structured around collection reveals, bespoke inquiry, and gallery-level image treatment.
- Home grammar: Collection gallery
- Mobile rule: Convert the collection view into clean stacked cards with direct bespoke and consult entry.

## 2. Visual Direction
- Family: Collection gallery commerce
- First screen: Gallery-led hero with macro product imagery and a clean consult CTA block.
- Background: High-resolution jewelry imagery, chrome reflections, and soft physical depth.
- Motion: Collection panels and consult cards rise with subtle metallic sheen.
- Atmosphere: Gallery polish, metallic depth, and premium restraint.

## 3. Palette
- Background: #d8dee8
- Surface: #f8fbff
- Panel: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)
- Text: #151b26
- Muted: #5d6879
- Accent: #1c2430
- Accent soft: #bcc6d6

## 4. Typography
- Precise, polished typography with clean spacing.
- Keep copy short and functional.
- Use Korean as the default content language.

## 5. Components
- Collection panels should feel reflective and deliberate.
- Shared primitives are limited to buttons, badges, inputs, and simple cards.
- The first screen grammar must be unique to this site.

## 6. Prohibited Patterns
- No shared hero template.
- No shared section assembler.
- No repetitive image box + text box + CTA blocks.

## 7. Route Targets
- Home (home): This is the operating homepage. Build a complete landing surface, not a cover page.
- Collection (collection): Use a browse or catalog structure with filtering, category anchors, and fast exploration.
- Bespoke (bespoke): Lead with product, room, program, or service depth. Make the decision points obvious.
- Consult (consult): Show an intentional conversion flow with options, quantity, summary, and next action.
- Brand (brand): Present the brand world, trust layer, and operating context rather than generic about text.
