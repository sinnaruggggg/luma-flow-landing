# Design System: PAPER NOOK
**Site ID:** indie-bookstore

## 1. Identity
- Industry: Independent Bookstore
- Summary: A curated bookstore site that should feel like an editorial shelf, not a mass retailer.
- Home grammar: Shelf editorial
- Mobile rule: Treat picks, notes, and gift CTA like a magazine deck rather than a compressed grid.

## 2. Visual Direction
- Family: Paper editorial commerce
- First screen: Shelf-and-note first screen with editorial snippets and gift cues.
- Background: Paper texture, torn edges, and warm photography rather than polished commerce blocks.
- Motion: Book notes and shelf cards float like clipped paper pieces.
- Atmosphere: Paper texture, editorial warmth, and tactile composition.

## 3. Palette
- Background: #f7f1e6
- Surface: #fffaf2
- Panel: rgba(255, 250, 242, 0.9)
- Text: #231d17
- Muted: #6d6154
- Accent: #f5c24f
- Accent soft: #9fd7f6

## 4. Typography
- Type should feel curated, note-like, and slightly hand-placed.
- Keep copy short and functional.
- Use Korean as the default content language.

## 5. Components
- Use collage overlap and clipped-paper behavior.
- Shared primitives are limited to buttons, badges, inputs, and simple cards.
- The first screen grammar must be unique to this site.

## 6. Prohibited Patterns
- No shared hero template.
- No shared section assembler.
- No repetitive image box + text box + CTA blocks.

## 7. Route Targets
- Home (home): This is the operating homepage. Build a complete landing surface, not a cover page.
- Shelf (shelf): Use a browse or catalog structure with filtering, category anchors, and fast exploration.
- Picks (picks): Lead with product, room, program, or service depth. Make the decision points obvious.
- Gift (gift): Show an intentional conversion flow with options, quantity, summary, and next action.
- Brand (brand): Present the brand world, trust layer, and operating context rather than generic about text.
