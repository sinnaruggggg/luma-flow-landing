# Design System: TABLE WARM
**Site ID:** local-cafe

## 1. Identity
- Industry: Cafe Reservation
- Summary: A cafe site focused on today's menu, seat availability, and direct reservation entry.
- Home grammar: Cafe window
- Mobile rule: Put today's menu and seat reservation in the first two blocks with no extra ceremony.

## 2. Visual Direction
- Family: Warm storefront booking
- First screen: Storefront hero with today's menu board and reservation module visible immediately.
- Background: Big cafe imagery, paper menu notes, and gentle hand-crafted depth.
- Motion: Menu plates and reservation notes lift softly over the storefront image.
- Atmosphere: Local storefront warmth with useful, lived-in energy.

## 3. Palette
- Background: #f4ead9
- Surface: #fff8ef
- Panel: rgba(255, 248, 239, 0.86)
- Text: #2b221a
- Muted: #6a5c4f
- Accent: #5d3b24
- Accent soft: #e8c498

## 4. Typography
- Mix compact labels with practical, readable hierarchy.
- Keep copy short and functional.
- Use Korean as the default content language.

## 5. Components
- Menu and booking blocks should feel hand-placed but clean.
- Shared primitives are limited to buttons, badges, inputs, and simple cards.
- The first screen grammar must be unique to this site.

## 6. Prohibited Patterns
- No shared hero template.
- No shared section assembler.
- No repetitive image box + text box + CTA blocks.

## 7. Route Targets
- Home (home): This is the operating homepage. Build a complete landing surface, not a cover page.
- Menu (menu): Use a browse or catalog structure with filtering, category anchors, and fast exploration.
- Visit (visit): Lead with product, room, program, or service depth. Make the decision points obvious.
- Reserve (reserve): Design around booking or request flow, availability, and frictionless form completion.
- Info (info): Organize operational guidance, visit rules, maps, FAQ, and preparation details clearly.
