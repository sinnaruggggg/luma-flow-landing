# Design System: MOSS HOME
**Site ID:** furniture-store

## 1. Identity
- Industry: Furniture Commerce
- Summary: A furniture site centered on room planning, bundle shopping, and spatial exploration.
- Home grammar: Room planner
- Mobile rule: Surface room choices and planner CTA as the main behavior, not as secondary footer content.

## 2. Visual Direction
- Family: Spatial planning commerce
- First screen: Room planner-led homepage with room selectors and bundle CTA visible immediately.
- Background: Large interior imagery, soft organic gradients, and layered planner panels.
- Motion: Room tags and planner cards float in like layout sheets.
- Atmosphere: Spatial, calm, and room-oriented with soft gradients.

## 3. Palette
- Background: #eef5e7
- Surface: #f9fcf4
- Panel: rgba(249, 252, 244, 0.84)
- Text: #23301f
- Muted: #55674f
- Accent: #24311f
- Accent soft: #cfe6bf

## 4. Typography
- Use smooth hierarchy and generous spacing.
- Keep copy short and functional.
- Use Korean as the default content language.

## 5. Components
- Planner and room selectors should feel soft and tactile.
- Shared primitives are limited to buttons, badges, inputs, and simple cards.
- The first screen grammar must be unique to this site.

## 6. Prohibited Patterns
- No shared hero template.
- No shared section assembler.
- No repetitive image box + text box + CTA blocks.

## 7. Route Targets
- Home (home): This is the operating homepage. Build a complete landing surface, not a cover page.
- Rooms (rooms): Use a browse or catalog structure with filtering, category anchors, and fast exploration.
- Planner (planner): Lead with product, room, program, or service depth. Make the decision points obvious.
- Cart (cart): Show an intentional conversion flow with options, quantity, summary, and next action.
- Service (service): Present the brand world, trust layer, and operating context rather than generic about text.
